. 3-bid Requirements for Major Expenditures:

a. For non-routine capital replacement or new equipment expenditures over $20,000, staff
will solicit bids from at least three vendors. Staff will document this process and their
final choice of vendor.

b. For ongoing large expenditures, such as unit renovations, staff will solicit bids for
planned expenditures in excess of $150,000 on a biennial basis. Staff will document this
process and their final choice of vendor.
