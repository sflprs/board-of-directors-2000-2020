Investment Report — Mr. Gridley reviewed the endowment portfolio performance through
November. The portfolio is up 15.9% on the year. Considering the significant increase
in the portfolio if the Van Ness Avenue property is sold, the Investment Committee met
with the investment manger, Allied Bernstein in early December. The Committee

remains satisfied with the performance and service of the incumbent manager and
recommended that The Heritage continue with this one firm. The investment strategy
will be to: maintain the current asset mix, cap target withdrawals at 3% annually and
maintain some liquidity and flexibility in the portfolio pending the clarification of the
strategic direction of the organization.
