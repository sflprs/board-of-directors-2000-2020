Public Charity/Exempt Foundation Status

Mr. Morris briefed the Board on discussions he held with attorney Filmore Rose, and the CPA firm
regarding the Public Charity status of the organization. The Heritage Investment Income is expect-
ed to exceed 1/3 of total income in the near future. As a public charity, this income would be sub-
ject to a 1-2% excise tax. The Heritage appears to be eligible to apply for Exempt Private Operat-
ing Foundation status, which is not subject to this tax. A motion was made by James Sherman,
seconded by Lewis Gridley, and passed unanimously, authorizing Mr. Morris to engage a legal

firm and CPA firm to advise the organization on the most appropriate status for The Society.
