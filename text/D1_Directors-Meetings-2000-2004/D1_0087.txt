Gifts Commitee

Pam Tudor reported that a gift solicitation letter will be sent in November. A key first step to a
successful fundraising program is a committed Board with 100% participation as donors.

Copies of an updated gifts list were distributed for review and corrections and each Board
member was asked to add 3-5 names to the list.

The Gifts Committee has recognized a need for help in putting together a fund development plan
and toward this end will be interviewing three consultants. Jane Hickerson and Carol Livingston
will participate in the interviews of fund raising consultants. At the December Board meeting,

the committee hopes to present a proposal from one of the consultants.

Mrs. Tudor expressed her appreciation to the members of the Gifts Committee, George Morris,
Elizabeth Mitchum, Martha Nell Beatty, Carolyn Barber, Barbara McGinley and Gayle Reynolds.
