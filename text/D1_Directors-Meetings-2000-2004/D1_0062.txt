September 11. She advised the Board that the Executive Committee asked Laura Pilz to
Stay on as Interim Director until October 25. Considering the number of projects in
Progress and concerns the staff have expressed about operating without a director, the
Executive Committee determined this would be in the best interest of The Heritage.
