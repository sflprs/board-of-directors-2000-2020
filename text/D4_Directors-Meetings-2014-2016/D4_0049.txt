Discrimination in the Dining Room:

He called attention to the article in the Board packet -summarizing recent Department
of Justice rulings regarding discriminating against residents from the Health Center
who want to take meals in the dining room. This issue has increased with the number
of Medicare patients who meet existing criteria and have shorter stays in the Health
Center. A clear policy is needed and educating Heritage residents is important. The
issue will be discussed in more detail at a later time.
