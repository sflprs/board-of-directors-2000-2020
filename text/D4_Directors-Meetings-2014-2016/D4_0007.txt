V. Resident Communications and Relations

Martha Nell Beatty reported that the 2017 slate of Resident Council officers will be
herself as President, Katherine Conley as Vice-President, Barbara Anderson as
Secretary and Patsy Hulting as Treasurer. She also reported on activities and events
that have allowed the Council to give a nice donation to the Employee Appreciation
Fund. Finally, she is working with LeadingAge California contacts in Sacramento to
arrange a visit from one of our State politicians sometime in December.
