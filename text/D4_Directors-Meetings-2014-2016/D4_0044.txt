T. Otstott attended sessions on fundraising, outreach, Grant Writing Do’s and Don'ts,
and the Boomer consumer. She reported that fundraising involves everyone - Board,
Leadership and Staff. It is an “all hands on deck” philosophy. Outreach is most
successful when collaborating and partnering to become a greater resource to the
