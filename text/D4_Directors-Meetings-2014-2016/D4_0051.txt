Il. Consent Agenda

The Board approved the Consent Agenda (August Board Minutes, Committee and Task
Team Reports, July Financial Snapshot, Operations Report, Marketing Report) ona
motion from M. Hauser and second by R. Gridley.

full resident capacity.
