Actuarial Report for The Heritage
M.A. V. Powell of A. V. Powell Associates presented to the board the firm’s actuarial study
completed and delivered this month. The report was produced to comply with regulations
that require life care communities file an actuarial study with the Department of Social
Services every five years. It also included an expanded format recommended by Dan Gray to
help the board make decisions on the pricing of The Heritage’s current and possible new
services. Summary:
" Deemed in satisfactory actuarial balance
* Reserve levels are in the highest quartile when compared to other similar facilities
Nursing units available for general community
Fees must at least match internal inflation
Cash should more than double in 10 years
* No imminent financial crisis
