Resident Communications and Relations

« Carolyn Barber reported on the May Residents’ Council Executive Committee
meeting, on behalf of the Residents’ Liaison Committee, (Elizabeth Stanners, Carolyn
Barber). She stated that the residents are highly interested in health and wellness,
healthier menu options, and house efficiencies, such as energy conservation and
recycling. This is reflected in: a desire to know more about the nutrition of food
served and limiting food portions; new walk opportunities in the neighborhood;
action taken to replace a stockpot with a new wok; and discussing changes of the
dinner hour that would include a buffet 5:30 to 6:00 PM and optional dinner service
from 6:00-7:00 PM. The residents are continuing to work towards more energy
savings and expanding recycling efforts.
