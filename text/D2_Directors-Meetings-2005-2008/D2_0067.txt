Approval of Board Conflict of Interest Policy

At the recommendation of The Heritage auditors, the Board approved a Board
Conflict of Interest Policy. This was signed by Board members, department heads
and employees who are involved in purchasing or arranging for services.

Although James Wheary has resigned from the Board, he will continue to serve on
the Audit Committee. Randy Gridley also volunteered to join the committee.
