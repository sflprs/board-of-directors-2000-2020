Update on Contiguous Property at 1530 Francisco Street
David Gruber reported that he provided comparable property information to the owner of 1530
Francisco Street. He will meet with the owner in January to continue the discussion about The
Heritage acquiring the property.

The Board had a brief discussion about the goal for property acquisition. It was generally agreed
that selective acquisition of additional property in proximity to 3400 Laguna Street is a good
investment diversification and provides flexibility for future needs.
