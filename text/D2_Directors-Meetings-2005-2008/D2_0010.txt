Vi. Facilities Committee
G. Ivelich reported that unit upgrades continue as independent living units turnover.
The dining room refurbishment, new carpet and paint, will be done in early
November. Residents will use the Theater room and Craft room for dining during the
10 days of construction. The Facilities Committee has applied to the city fora
meeting with Planning Department staff. The purpose of the meeting is to have a
general discussion of the height, density and scope of changes The Heritage can
consider making to the property.

L. Van Houten raised the question of how this remodel addresses the changing
demographics of senior demand. Ivelich indicated that the discussions are very
general at this stage and there will be an opportunity to analyze demand more
completely after the meeting with the city.

D. Gruber reported that the purchase of the Francisco Street flats is on hold at present.

L. Pilz reported that the committee has narrowed the Community Grant applications
