Financial Report

Operations Report: The Board unanimously approved 1) The Heritage 2008 Operating Budget
onamotion by Molly Hauser, seconded by Randy Gridley, and 2) The Heritage 2008 Capital
Budget on a motion by Molly Hauser, seconded by George Morris.

Investment Report: Randy Gridley reported that 2007 was the first year the investment portfolio
did not beat the benchmark due to the portfolio tilt towards value stocks. While value performs
better than growth over the long term, last year growth outperformed. The portfolio is conserva-
tively weighted with 30% in bonds and 70% in equities. Bernstein Global Wealth Management,
the portfolio’s manager, will hold its annual review on February 13, at 3:30 PM. All Board
members are encouraged to attend. Board members are also encouraged to attend the Bernstein
Annual Meeting in March. Randy Gridley will be certain all Board members receive invitations.
