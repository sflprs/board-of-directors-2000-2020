Chair D. Gruber called the meeting to order at 4:08 PM.

IL Introduction of Raygenia Stewart, Director of Nursing

D.Gruber welcomed to The Heritage Raygenia Stewart, new Director of Nursing (DON),

who had served as interim for the past nine months through GeriHealth Solutions, before
taking the position permanently. He expressed appreciation to The Heritage Medical
Director, Dr. Maryam Matin and Board Member H. Safrit for their role in encouraging her to
apply. R. Stewart spoke to her priorities for the Health Center, among them implementing
electronic health records and standards for potential Medicare licensing.
