Vil. Development Committee Update

Pamela De Martini reported that $100,000 in gifts were received in 2010, the highest
number of gifts in over a decade. Generous gifts were received from several residents’
estates, relatives of residents, the Kimball Foundation and the Barrett and Margaret Hindes
Foundation. Residents were not asked to participate in the year end appeal as appreciation
for putting up with all the construction. They will be thanked for their patience with a
Board tea in January.
