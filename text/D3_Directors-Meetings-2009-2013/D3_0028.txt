VI. Medicare Certification Presentation

HES Consultant L. Blitz reviewed the issues regarding Medicare Certification of the Health Center.
The Board approved an additional $20,000 to proceed with Phase II on a motion from C. Tiret and
second from G. Morris. Phase II will refine the costs and time frame, assess staff training, detail
billing procedures, and identify regulatory issues of OSHPD (Office of Statewide Health Planning
and Development). After presentation of Phase II results at the October meeting, the Board will
decide to proceed or not with Medicare Certification.
