VI. Other Business - Medicare Compliance

As required by federally funded Medicare, a resolution to adopt a Corporate Compliance
Plan was approved by the Board of Directors on a motion from D. Gruber and second
from A. Carlson. The manual sets forth a code of conduct, ethics, policies and pro-
cedures, education, training, compliance regulations among other guidelines.
