Chair L. Pilz called the meeting to order at 4:00 PM.

I. Approval of Consent Agenda

The August 28, 2013, minutes were approved by the Board on a motion from C. Barber
and second by R. Gridley. No questions were raised in regard to the items on the
Consent Agenda (Development/Marketing Committee Minutes,, August Financial
Snapshot, Executive Director’s Report). P. Tudor drew the Board's attention to the
Development/ Marketing Committee Minutes and mention of a Case Statement for a
Multi-functional/Garden Room/ Solarium prepared by the Committee with staff input that
was sent to the Master Plan Task Team September 14. The Committee’s Case State-
ment requested that the Task Team consider placing the Multi-functional Garden Room/
Solarium on the priority list of master plan projects to be addressed in early 2014.
